# SasyamAI Agricultural & Agronomy Advisory Guide

This guide contains core agronomic principles, seasonal calendars, crop-soil compatibility rules, Integrated Pest Management (IPM), and fertilizer guidance for Indian farming conditions.

---

## 1. Indian Cropping Seasons

### A. Kharif Season (Monsoon: June – October)
- **Key Crops**: Paddy (Rice), Cotton, Maize, Soybean, Groundnut, Pigeon Pea (Arhar/Tur), Green Gram (Moong), Black Gram (Urad), Bajra, Jowar.
- **Characteristics**: Sown at the onset of monsoon rains; requires substantial moisture, high temperatures, and humid weather.
- **Key Considerations**: Adequate drainage in black soil, nursery preparation for rice in May-June, seed inoculation with Rhizobium for pulses.

### B. Rabi Season (Winter: October – March)
- **Key Crops**: Wheat, Mustard, Gram (Chickpea), Barley, Potato, Peas, Lentil, Onion, Cumin, Coriander.
- **Characteristics**: Sown after monsoon retreat; requires cool temperature during growth and warm sunny weather during ripening/harvesting.
- **Key Considerations**: Pre-sowing irrigation (Paleva), timely weed control (within 30-35 days), balanced fertilizer application (NPK + Zinc for wheat).

### C. Zaid Season (Summer: March – June)
- **Key Crops**: Watermelon, Muskmelon, Cucumber, Bitter Gourd, Okra (Bhindi), Fodder crops, Moong (Summer).
- **Characteristics**: Grown between Rabi and Kharif in irrigated tracts; warm, dry climate with long day lengths.
- **Key Considerations**: Drip irrigation / frequent light irrigation, mulch application to conserve soil moisture.

## 2. Soil Types & Agro-Climatic Suitability in India

| Soil Type | Key Regions | Properties | Suitable Crops | Management Tips |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Alluvial Soil** | Indo-Gangetic Plains (Punjab, Haryana, UP, Bihar, WB), River deltas | Rich in potash, deficient in nitrogen & phosphorus, high water retention | Wheat, Rice, Sugarcane, Maize, Pulses, Oilseeds, Vegetables | Add organic compost, apply balanced NPK, rotate with legumes. |
| **Black Soil (Regur)** | Deccan plateau (Maharashtra, MP, Gujarat, Karnataka, AP, Telangana) | High clay content, self-ploughing cracks in dry season, high moisture retention | Cotton, Soybean, Wheat, Jowar, Onion, Sugarcane, Citrus fruits | Avoid waterlogging during heavy rains, deep summer ploughing. |
| **Red & Yellow Soil** | Tamil Nadu, Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, parts of Karnataka | Porous, slightly acidic, low in nitrogen, phosphorus, and humus | Groundnut, Millets (Ragi, Bajra), Pulses, Tobacco, Potato, Oilseeds | Apply lime for acidic soils, incorporate green manure, use drip irrigation. |
| **Laterite Soil** | Western Ghats, Eastern Ghats, Kerala, Assam | Highly leached, acidic, poor in fertility and organic matter | Cashew, Tea, Coffee, Rubber, Coconut, Tapioca, Arecanut | Heavy application of farmyard manure (FYM), bio-fertilizers, contour bunding. |
| **Sandy / Arid Soil** | Rajasthan, parts of Gujarat, South Punjab | High permeability, low water retention, low nutrients | Bajra, Guar, Moth bean, Mustard, Cumin, Date palm, Pomegranate | Use micro-irrigation (Drip/Sprinkler), mulching, addition of organic matter. |

## 3. Integrated Nutrient Management (INM) & Soil Health

- **NPK Ratio Principles**:
  - Cereals (Wheat, Rice, Maize): Standard N:P:K target is 4:2:1 (e.g., 120:60:40 kg/ha).
  - Pulses (Chickpea, Moong, Urad): 1:2:1 (e.g., 20:40:20 kg/ha) — pulses fix their own nitrogen via root nodules.
  - Oilseeds (Mustard, Groundnut): 3:2:1 + Sulphur (20-30 kg/ha) for oil content.
- **Micronutrients**:
  - Zinc deficiency (Khaira disease in rice, little leaf in citrus): Apply Zinc Sulphate 21% @ 25 kg/ha or spray 0.5% ZnSO4 + 0.25% lime.
  - Boron deficiency (cracking in tomato/pomegranate): Spray 0.1-0.2% Solubor during flowering.
- **Organic Enrichment**:
  - Apply 5-10 tonnes/acre Well-decomposed Farm Yard Manure (FYM) or 2 tonnes/acre Vermicompost before final land preparation.
  - Use *Jeevambutha* / *Beejamrutha* for microbial activation and seed treatment.

## 4. Integrated Pest & Disease Management (IPM)

1. **Cultural Methods**: Crop rotation, deep summer ploughing (kills dormant pupae and fungal spores), intercropping (e.g., Marigold with Tomato to deter nematodes).
2. **Biological Controls**:
   - Install pheromone traps (@ 5-8 traps/acre for bollworms, shoot & fruit borer).
   - Install yellow & blue sticky traps (@ 10-15 traps/acre for whiteflies, thrips, aphids).
   - Spray Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%) or Azadirachtin 10,000 ppm @ 2-3 ml/L for sucking pests.
   - Trichoderma viride / Pseudomonas fluorescens @ 5-10 g/kg seed or 2.5 kg/acre soil application for fungal root rot / wilt control.
3. **Targeted Remedial Measures**:
   - Sucking pests (Aphids, Whiteflies, Thrips): Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 0.5 ml/L or Thiamethoxam 25 WG @ 0.3 g/L.
   - Fungal Leaf Spots & Blights: Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/L or Azoxystrobin + Difenoconazole @ 1 ml/L.
   - Powdery Mildew: Wettable Sulphur 80 WP @ 2.5 g/L or Hexaconazole 5% EC @ 1 ml/L.

## 5. Smart Irrigation & Water Conservation

- **Drip Irrigation**: Recommended for cotton, sugarcane, vegetables, fruit orchards; saves 40-60% water and boosts yield by 20-30%.
- **Sprinkler Irrigation**: Ideal for undulating terrain, pulses, wheat, mustard, groundnut.
- **Critical Growth Stages for Irrigation**:
  - Wheat: Crown Root Initiation (CRI at 21 days), Tillering, Flowering, Milking stage.
  - Rice: Panicle initiation, Flowering, Grain filling (maintain saturated soil, avoid deep standing water unnecessarily).
  - Maize: Tasseling and Silking stages (water stress severely hampers yield).
